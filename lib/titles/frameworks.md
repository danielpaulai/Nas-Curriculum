# Title Frameworks — Extended Library

The 8 core frameworks plus 2 new Nas.com School frameworks (added May 2026 post-pivot). This file documents the full library plus advanced patterns.

## Quick Reference

| # | Name | Pattern | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MAGIC | [Verb] [Outcome] [Tool] [Time] | Default. Always start here. |
| 2 | Transformation | From X to Y: [how] | Story-driven sessions, journeys |
| 3 | Turn X Into Y | Turn [common] Into [valuable] | Repurposing / leverage topics |
| 4 | Outcome-That-Sells | [Verb] [Outcome] That [Verb] | Marketing/conversion (use sparingly with "sales") |
| 5 | Negation Hook | [X] Without [hard] / Even if [Y] | Beginner-friendly / skeptic-converting |
| 6 | Specificity Stack | [#] [Unit] in [time] with [tool] | Tactical, batch-output sessions |
| 7 | Question Hook | Why/How/What [pain]? | Use sparingly. Diagnostic sessions. |
| 8 | Roast/Live-Build | Roasting Your X / [Action] Live | Nas signature live-build format |
| **9** | **Market-Type-Specific** ⭐ | **Market your [product type] on [channel]** | **Post-pivot DEFAULT for commerce sessions** |
| **10** | **Sales-Channel-Specific** ⭐ | **Get your first [N] customers on [channel]** | **Post-pivot DEFAULT for channel sessions** |

## Framework Detail

### 1. MAGIC (Hormozi $100M Offers)

The five elements:
- **M**agnetic reason why — implicit (the verb does this)
- **A**vatar — implicit ("your" / "you")
- **G**oal — concrete outcome the attendee leaves with
- **I**nterval — specific timeframe
- **C**ontainer — the method/tool delivering it

Template: `[Verb] [Your] [Specific Outcome] [in/with/using] [Tool/Method] [in Time]`

Examples:
- Build Your AI Sales Agent Live in 60 Minutes
- Create a Full Brand Kit in 10 Minutes With AI
- Build Your Entire Store Using AI in One Session
- Design Your Brand in 30 Minutes Using Canva AI

### 2. Transformation (Caples / Halbert lineage)

Template: `From [Start State] to [End State][: optional how]`

Examples:
- From Chat to Cash: How to Sell Services Using Only ChatGPT and WhatsApp
- From Invisible to Unforgettable: Design a Customer Journey That Converts
- From $0 to $10K: Build Your AI Consulting Business
- From Idea to Demo: Vibe-Code Your AI Product to Life
- From Voice Clone to Full Episodes: Master AI Podcast Production

Trigger: when the session is about a journey or before/after.

### 3. Turn X Into Y (Nas-signature)

Template: `Turn [Common thing user has] Into [Valuable thing they want]`

Examples:
- Turn Your DMs Into Sales Using AI Automation
- Turn Any Idea Into a $100K Business in 90 Minutes
- Turn 100 Leads Into 20 Sales Meetings
- Turn One Video Into 20 Pieces of Content
- Turn Failed Products Into Your Next Big Winner Using AI Analysis
- Turn Existing Content Into Paid Offers in One Session

Strongest variant of all 8 patterns based on corpus frequency. Use whenever input matches the leverage shape (X → Y where Y is more valuable).

### 4. Outcome-That-Sells

Template: `[Verb] [Specific Outcome Noun] That [Sells/Converts/Closes/Drives/Earns]`

Examples:
- Build a Product Ad That Converts in 60 Minutes With Claude
- Write Sales Copy That Actually Sells
- Create AI Sales Funnels That Close Deals Without You
- Create Meme Ads With AI That Actually Sell
- Build an AI Sales Agent That Replies and Closes Leads 24/7
- Build a YouTube Channel That Earns Without You Ever Appearing On Camera

Trigger: anything sales/marketing/ads/conversion adjacent.

### 5. Negation Hook

Template A: `[Outcome] Without [Hard/Annoying Thing]`
Template B: `[Outcome] (Even If [Disqualifier])`
Template C: `[Outcome] (Zero Code Required)` / `(No Coding)`

Examples:
- Build High-Converting Landing Pages in 15 Minutes (Zero Code Required)
- Sell Without the Sleazy Sales Tactics
- Go Viral on TikTok Without Showing Your Face
- Design Your Brand in 30 Minutes (Even if You're Not a Designer)
- Build and Launch a Software Product Without Writing a Single Line of Code With Lovable

Trigger: beginner-friendly sessions, sessions that bust an assumed barrier.

### 6. Specificity Stack

Template: `[Number] [Concrete Unit] [Action/Tool] [Timeframe]`

Examples:
- Create 30 Short Product Videos and a Posting Plan That Drives Sales
- Build 5 AI Sales Personas in 60 Minutes
- Turn 1 Script Into 5 Video Styles Using AI
- Create 30 Days of Content in 90 Minutes Without Guessing
- Create 50 Viral Video Hooks With AI (Live Hook Factory)

Trigger: batch-output sessions, repeatable systems.

### 7. Question Hook

Template: `Why/What/How [Pain Point]?`  *or*  `[Question]: [Resolution Promise]`

Examples:
- Why Nobody Is Buying Your Product (And How to Fix It in 30 Minutes)
- What's Killing Your Conversion Rate? Find Out Live
- Why Your AI Outputs Look Like Everyone Else's

Use sparingly. **Max 1 question title per batch of 5.** Questions are weaker by default in this brand.

### 8. Roast / Live-Build (Nas Signature Format)

Template A: `Roasting Your [Thing] - [Promise / Outcome]`
Template B: `[Build/Action] Live in [Timeframe]`
Template C: `[Action] Together - [Container/Stake]`

Examples:
- Roasting Your Digital Products - What Works vs What Flops
- Roasting Your Personal Brand - Bring Your LinkedIn
- Roasting Your AI Videos - Turn Good Into Great
- Build Your AI Sales Agent Live in 60 Minutes
- Build a Digital Product Together - AI School Puts $100 Behind It

Trigger: live-format sessions, audience-participation sessions, peer-feedback sessions.

### 9. Market-Type-Specific ⭐ (NEW — Nas.com School default for commerce)

Template A: `Market Your [Product Type] on [Channel] in [Time]`
Template B: `Market Your [Product Type] Without [Common Pain]`
Template C: `Sell More [Product Type] Using AI [Tool / Method]`

Product types to name: print-on-demand, dropshipping, handmade, high-ticket, low-ticket, subscription, digital, physical, t-shirt, jewelry, ebook, course, service.

Channels to name: TikTok Shop, Instagram, Etsy, Amazon, Facebook, Pinterest, Shopify, Nas.com, LinkedIn.

Examples:
- Market Your Print-on-Demand Product on Instagram in 60 Minutes
- Market Your Dropshipping Product Without Looking Generic
- Market Your High-Ticket Product Without Sounding Salesy
- Market Your Handmade Jewelry to a Global Audience With AI
- Sell More T-Shirts Using AI-Generated UGC Ads
- Market Your Digital Course Without a Big Audience

Trigger: any commerce session. This should be the **first** framework you reach for post-May-2026. Replaces generic "Build X with AI" for e-commerce topics.

### 10. Sales-Channel-Specific ⭐ (NEW — Nas.com School default for channels)

Template A: `Get Your First [N] [Customers/Orders/Sales] on [Channel]`
Template B: `Open Your [Channel] and [Outcome] in [Time]`
Template C: `Build a [Channel] Storefront That [Marketing Outcome]`

Examples:
- Get Your First 100 Customers on TikTok Shop
- Get Your First Order on Etsy Within 30 Days
- Open Your TikTok Shop and Land Your First Order This Week
- Build an Instagram Storefront That Earns Daily Orders
- Find Your First 50 Customers on Facebook Groups (No Spam)
- Build an Amazon Listing That Outranks Your Competitors

Trigger: any session about a specific sales channel (TikTok Shop, Etsy, Amazon, Instagram, Facebook Marketplace, etc.).

**Note on the word "Sales"**: Patterns 4, 9, and 10 may seem like they need the word "sales" — they don't. Use "customers", "orders", "buyers", "browsers", "income" instead. "Sales" depresses RSVP per corpus data.

## Advanced: Subtitle / Series Pattern

For multi-part series, use the colon split:

`[Hook Title] - Part [N]: [Specific subtopic]`

Examples:
- Master Any Topic in 60 Minutes Using AI Research Techniques - Part 1: Foundation
- Create AI Sales Funnels That Close Deals Without You - Part 1: Setup & Strategy
- Launch a Marketing Campaign That Drives Sales - Part 2: The AI Content Engine

The hook title MUST stand alone. The subtitle adds the breakdown.

## Advanced: The Curiosity Comma

`[Plain Statement], [Surprise Reframe]`

Examples:
- Turn One Video Into 20 Pieces of Content, Your AI Content Team Handles It

Use rarely — only when the reframe genuinely adds curiosity.

## Pattern Frequencies in Corpus (for context)

From analyzing 432 historical titles:
- "Build X" pattern: 12.3%
- "Turn X Into Y": 6.9%
- "From X to Y": 2.3%
- "In N minutes/hours/days": 5.8%
- "Without X": 3.0%
- Colon split (subtitle): 18.3%
- Has number/figure: 38%
- Has named tool: 24%
- "AI" in title: 45.6% (yes, AI is in the corpus — the rule isn't to avoid the word "AI", it's to avoid AI-slop *language*)
